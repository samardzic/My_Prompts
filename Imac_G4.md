```text
PMU reset procedure
Because your iMac is already open:
1. Shut the iMac down.
2. Unplug the AC power cord completely.
3. Wait a few seconds.
4. Locate the small PMU RESET button on the logic board.
   - On the iMac G4 it is on the bottom side of the logic board, near the test points.
   - It may be underneath a small protective cover.
5. Press the PMU reset button exactly once.
   - A brief press is sufficient.
   - Do NOT press it twice.
   - Apple's service doc explicitly warns that pressing it a second time can crash the PMU.
6. Wait at least 10 seconds.
7. Reconnect the AC power cord.
8. Power on the iMac.
That's it.

Important for your particular situation
You do not need the HDD, CD drive, keyboard, or mouse connected for this test.
```

The hairdryer-trick only worked one time for me. At least I knew for sure now that the power supply is defective and nothing else.
So: I finally repaired my iMac G4 15" (first edition 2002).
All I had to do is to change these 3 capacitors on the power supply board.
C212, C215, C216
It's not easy to unscrew this tight baby, but it was worth it!
Please google for "imac g4 c212 c215 c216" for further instructions.
