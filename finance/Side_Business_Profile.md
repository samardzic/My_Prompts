# Side Business Profile and Strategic Direction

## 1. Objective

The primary objective is to build a **scalable side business** that can eventually generate substantial additional income and potentially become a significant independent source of revenue.

The initial target is approximately:

- **€1,000/month** in additional income.

However, this is not the ultimate objective. The primary goal is to identify and develop a business with the potential to scale considerably beyond this level.

The business should ideally:

- Start with relatively low capital requirements.
- Be developed alongside full-time employment.
- Require approximately 2–3 hours of work per day initially.
- Have potential for automation.
- Be scalable without requiring a proportional increase in personal workload.
- Eventually generate income with relatively limited ongoing operational involvement.
- Allow experimentation before committing significant capital.
- Potentially expand into international markets.
- Build an asset rather than simply exchanging time for money.

The preferred approach is to **experiment with several business opportunities, validate them with limited investment, and concentrate resources on the most promising ones**.

## 2. Financial Profile

### Available Capital

Initial available capital:

- Approximately €5,000–€7,000.

This capital is available for business experiments and potential investments, but it does not necessarily need to be deployed immediately.

The preferred strategy is to preserve capital until an opportunity has demonstrated sufficient potential.

### Initial Experiment Budget

Suggested budget for initial experiments:

- €100–€500 per experiment.

Potential investment in a validated opportunity:

- €500–€1,500.

Larger investments should be considered only after obtaining evidence of demand, profitability, and operational viability.

### Income Target

Initial additional income target:

- Approximately €1,000/month.

Longer-term objective:

- Develop a business capable of generating significantly more than €1,000/month.
- Ideally create a scalable business that could eventually generate €2,000–€5,000/month or more.

The income target is not the primary constraint during the initial experimentation phase.

The main priority is finding a business model with strong long-term potential.

### Financial Strategy

Preferred approach:

1. Research potential business opportunities.
2. Identify promising business models.
3. Conduct small experiments.
4. Measure actual results.
5. Eliminate unsuccessful ideas quickly.
6. Reinvest selectively in validated opportunities.
7. Scale gradually.

The initial capital should be treated as an experimentation and validation budget rather than money that must immediately be spent.

## 18. Final Objective

The ultimate objective is to build a scalable business that:

- Generates meaningful additional income.
- Has the potential to grow significantly.
- Does not require the user to exchange personal working hours directly for revenue.
- Can operate primarily online.
- Uses automation and AI wherever practical.
- Requires limited customer interaction.
- Does not depend on a personal brand.
- Can initially be developed alongside full-time employment.
