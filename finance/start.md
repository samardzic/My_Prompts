### Background and Objective

I’m interested in starting to invest in the stock market, primarily by purchasing stocks and potentially ETFs (Exchange-Traded Funds).
I’m a complete beginner in this area, so I would like a practical, step-by-step guide that takes me from having no experience to making my first small investment.
My intention is to start with relatively small amounts of money, learn how the process works, understand the risks, and gradually increase my investments as I gain experience.
I am based in Serbia, which is not an EU member state.
This is an important consideration because I need to understand which investment platforms, brokers, financial products, and regulatory frameworks are actually available to Serbian residents.

### 3. Choosing an Investment Platform

Identify reputable platforms accessible to Serbian residents.

* Availability to Serbian residents.
* Regulatory oversight and investor protection.

* Minimum initial deposit.
* Transaction fees and commissions.
* Currency conversion costs (EUR/RSD/USD).
* Deposit and withdrawal methods available from Serbian banks.
* Available stocks, ETFs, and other investment instruments.
* Account maintenance and inactivity fees.
* Ease of use for beginners.

Prioritize established, regulated platforms over speculative or less-established alternatives.

### 8. Risks and Things to Avoid

Explain the most common beginner mistakes:

* Investing money that might be needed soon.
* Concentrating investments in a single company.
* Panic selling during market downturns.
* Investing based on social media recommendations.
* Using leverage, margin, CFDs, or options without understanding the risks.
* Ignoring fees and taxes.
* Confusing investing with gambling.
