- Be realistic and objective.
- Do not recommend generic side hustles without explaining why they are suitable.

- Consider the practical realities of living and operating a business in Serbia.
- Prioritize ideas that can exploit my existing skills, knowledge, and interests, but do not restrict yourself to them.
- Think creatively and look for opportunities that are not immediately obvious.
- Where useful, use current market research rather than relying solely on general knowledge.
- Clearly separate facts, assumptions, and estimates.
- Focus on businesses that could realistically generate meaningful additional income while maintaining a full-time job.
- Consider the impact of AI and automation, but do not overestimate their capabilities.
- Pay attention to platform policies, copyright, monetization restrictions, and regulatory requirements.
- Prioritize opportunities that can be tested cheaply.
- Avoid committing significant capital before demand is validated.
