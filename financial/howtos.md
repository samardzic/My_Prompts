7. What are transaction costs?

Transaction costs are all expenses associated with executing and maintaining investments.

Typical examples:

| Cost                 | Explanation                           |
|:---------------------|:--------------------------------------|
| Brokerage commission | Fee for buying/selling                |
| Currency conversion  | Converting EUR into USD, for example  |
| Bank transfer        | Sending money to your broker          |
| Exchange fees        | Fees charged by exchanges             |
| Regulatory fees      | Certain mandatory transaction charges |
| ETF expense ratio    | Annual operating cost of the ETF      |
| Withdrawal fee       | Fee for withdrawing money             |
